Have these ready to tell:

1. **"Tell me about a complex system you built"**
   → Gloria pipeline: ingestion → dedup → classification → enrichment → curation → generation → QA

2. **"How do you evaluate AI models?"**
   → Built evaluation frameworks comparing GPT-4, GPT-4o, Gemini on accuracy, cost, and latency

3. **"How do you handle production AI challenges?"**
   → Cost optimization, false positive reduction, prompt engineering for deterministic outputs

4. **"What's your approach to product decisions?"**
   → Systems thinking: identify bottlenecks, design for failure modes, optimize for the metric that matters

5. **"Why are you leaving your current role?"**
   → "I've built the AI system from the ground up and I'm ready for a bigger challenge where I can have more impact at a company with more resources and ambition."
